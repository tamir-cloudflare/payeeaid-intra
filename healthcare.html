Frequently Asked Questions

When can I change my plan?+

You can change your plan during Open Enrollment (November 1–15) or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, divorce, birth/adoption, or loss of other coverage.

How do I find an in-network doctor?+

Visit cignahealthcare.com and search for providers in the Cigna Open Access Plus network. For international coverage, use the Cigna Global network directory. You can also call Cigna at 1-800-244-6224.

What is an HSA and how does it work?+

A Health Savings Account (HSA) lets you set aside pre-tax money for medical expenses. The Basic and Premium plans are HSA-eligible. PayeeAid contributes $1,000/year to your HSA. You own the account — it rolls over and is portable if you leave.

How do I submit a claim?+

Most in-network providers file claims directly. For out-of-network claims, submit via the Cigna member portal within 90 days of service. Include itemized bill and explanation of services.

What happens if I travel internationally?+

The Basic plan covers emergencies only. Premium covers full medical for 180 days/year abroad. Executive covers unlimited worldwide. Carry your Cigna Global insurance card when traveling. Call Cigna Global Customer Service collect at +1-302-467-4200 for assistance.
