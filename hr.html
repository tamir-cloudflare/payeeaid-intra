Company Policies

📋 Employee Code of Conduct +

All employees are expected to uphold PayeeAid's values of integrity, transparency, and respect in every interaction.

  • Professional conduct expected at all times — with colleagues, clients, and vendors
  • Confidentiality of client and company data is mandatory
  • Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to your manager and HR
  • Harassment or discrimination of any kind is strictly prohibited
  • Report violations to hr@payeeaid.com or via the anonymous hotline
🏖️ Leave & Time Off Policy +

PayeeAid offers generous leave to support work-life balance:

  • Annual PTO: 25 days/year (accrued monthly), carryover up to 5 days
  • Sick Leave: 10 days/year, no carryover
  • Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid for primary caregiver, 8 weeks for secondary
  • Bereavement: 5 days for immediate family, 2 days for extended
  • Public Holidays: 11 days per local office calendar
  • Birthday: 1 additional paid day off
  • All leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal with manager approval
🏠 Remote & Hybrid Work Policy +

PayeeAid supports flexible working arrangements:

  • Hybrid: Minimum 2 days in office per week for office-based roles
  • Remote: Fully remote roles must be approved by department head
  • Core hours: 10am–3pm local time must be available for meetings
  • Equipment: Company-provided laptop + monitor + headset for all staff
  • Home office stipend: $100/month for remote employees
  • International remote: Must comply with local tax and employment laws
💰 Compensation & Performance Reviews +

Annual compensation review cycle:

  • Performance reviews: Bi-annually (January and July)
  • Compensation review: Annually in January, effective February 1
  • Merit increases: Based on performance rating and budget pool
  • Promotion cycle: Reviewed in July cycle, effective October 1
  • Bonus: Annual performance bonus up to 15% of base salary
  • Equity: RSU grants for eligible roles, 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
  • Salary bands are published internally on the HR portal
🎓 Learning & Development +

Investing in your growth:

  • Annual budget: $2,500 per employee for external courses and certifications
  • LinkedIn Learning: Full access for all employees
  • Internal certifications: Compliance, security awareness, and product tracks
  • Conference attendance: Up to 2 conferences/year with manager approval
  • Mentorship program: Pair with senior leaders for career guidance
  • Internal transfers: After 12 months in current role, eligible to apply internally
🤝 Grievance & Disciplinary Procedure +

We are committed to a fair and transparent workplace:

  • Informal resolution: Encourage direct discussion with your manager first
  • Formal grievance: Submit in writing to hr@payeeaid.com
  • Investigation: HR investigates within 5 business days
  • Disciplinary steps: Verbal warning → Written warning → Final warning → Termination
  • Appeals: May appeal any decision to the People Operations Director within 10 days
  • Anonymous hotline: Available 24/7 for sensitive reports
